## What changed

- On April 17, 2026 the Federal Reserve, with the OCC and FDIC, issued SR 26-2, replacing SR 11-7 and SR 21-8 with a risk-based approach to model risk. [src-sr26-2]
- It expects to be most relevant to Federal Reserve-regulated banking organizations above $30 billion in total assets. [src-sr26-2]

## What is certain for this account

- OSFI Guideline E-23 covers model risk, including AI and machine learning models, and takes effect on May 1, 2027. [src-osfi-e23]
- E-23 expects an enterprise model inventory that includes vendor models, with monitoring for autonomous decision-making and drift. [src-osfi-e23]
- OSFI Guideline B-13 sets technology and cyber risk expectations for federally regulated financial institutions. [src-osfi-b13]

## What depends on structure (confirm)

- Confirm: SR 26-2 applicability requires confirmation; confirm whether Northbridge has a Federal Reserve-regulated banking organization. [src-sr26-2]
- Confirm: whether Northbridge has EU entities of the types DORA covers; that depends on entity type, jurisdiction and facts. [src-dora]
