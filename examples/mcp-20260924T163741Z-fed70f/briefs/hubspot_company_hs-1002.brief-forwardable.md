## What changed
- On April 17, 2026 the Federal Reserve, with the OCC and FDIC, issued SR 26-2, revised model risk management guidance that supersedes SR 11-7 and SR 21-8. [src-sr26-2]
- The letter asks for a risk-based approach tailored to each banking organization's model risk profile, size and complexity. [src-sr26-2]

## What is certain for this account
- The letter expects to be most relevant to banking organizations with over $30 billion in total assets regulated by the Federal Reserve. [src-sr26-2]
